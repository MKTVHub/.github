<p align="center"><b>Making every TV smart.</b></p>

🌐 **[mktvhub.github.io](https://mktvhub.github.io)**

Smart TVs ship with whatever their maker decided to give you. We build open-source apps that make any TV better: Android TV and Google TV today, then LG webOS, Samsung Tizen and others.

### Projects

| | |
|---|---|
| **[mkTV](https://github.com/MKTVHub/mkTV)** | A web browser built for the TV remote. Ad and tracker blocking, D-pad "jump" navigation, full-screen watch mode, YouTube TV mode, private browsing, and a phone remote you open by scanning a QR code. |

### What we care about

- **Built for the remote.** Every feature has to be easy with a D-pad, or with a phone.
- **Private by default.** No accounts, no analytics, no tracking.
- **Open.** Apache-2.0 code, clear docs, and `AGENTS.md` files so people and coding agents can contribute.
- **Every TV.** Platform-specific parts stay thin so the ideas can be ported.

### Get involved

Read [mkTV's CONTRIBUTING guide](https://github.com/MKTVHub/mkTV/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) and the [roadmap](https://github.com/MKTVHub/mkTV/blob/main/docs/ROADMAP.md). Ports to other TV platforms are very welcome.
